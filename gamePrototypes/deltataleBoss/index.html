Controls:

WASD: Movement and action selection
Enter: Select action
Space: Fire bullet while fire action is selected

Actions:

Claw: Do imidiate damage to the hydra
Fire: During the next attack you'll be able to fire bullets
Heal: You heal

About:

This was a quick Undertale/Deltarune style boss I put together. The main gimic is that the boss eventually begins to spin the player and therefore their controls. Once it begans turing you to random diagonals the fight becomes extremely difficult!
