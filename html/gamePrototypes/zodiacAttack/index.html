Controls:

Left & Right Arrow Keys or A & D: move
Space: Fire
Reload Page: restart after you die
Mouse: Control Tool
Click: Use Tool
Scroll wheel: Change the number in the top left (IDK what it's for)

About:

This was my first unity project. It is extremely basic but it works and it started down a great journey that eventually led me here. I believe this was made as a part of a game jam though I can't remember which one. according to the itch.io page, the story is the following:
"The bosses are just evil aliens that use zodiac signs to gain power. But, as time goes on, the zodiac signs start corrupting and combining with the aliens, making it even harder to de-attach them from the alien’s body. It is the player’s job to free the zodiac signs from the aliens. "
